# PRODUCT.md — The Adire Renova (TAR)

## What it is
E-commerce store for The Adire Renova (TAR), a hand-dyed adire and African dye-fabric business based in Kaduna, Nigeria. The storefront sells fabric by the piece and by the yard, ships nationwide in Nigeria, and takes payment in NGN via Paystack.

## Audience
- Primary: Nigerian shoppers (Lagos, Abuja, Kaduna, Port Harcourt), mobile-heavy, buying fabric for personal wear, tailoring, and events (weddings, aso-ebi groups).
- Secondary: diaspora and design-conscious buyers who value authentic hand-dyed textiles and want the story of the craft.

## Product truth
- Every fabric is hand-dyed in Kaduna; no two pieces are identical.
- Categories: adire eleko, kampala, oniko, alabere; sold in 2, 4, 6, and 8 yard lengths.
- Prices in naira (₦). Nationwide delivery. Contact via WhatsApp is a normal part of the buying culture.
- Brand palette is pinned: orange (primary action), green (brand depth), warm white (canvas).
- Tone: warm, confident, artisanal. Premium without being exclusive. Nigerian voice, no borrowed corporate polish.

## What the storefront must prove
1. The fabrics are beautiful and authentic — photography leads.
2. Buying is easy — clear product pages, fast cart, trusted Paystack checkout.
3. There are real hands behind the cloth — the craft story is one click from anywhere.

## Success
A first-time visitor understands within seconds: hand-dyed adire, made in Kaduna, buy now in naira. A shopper completes checkout on a mid-range Android phone over 3G.

## Constraints
- Mobile-first Nigeria traffic; performance budget is strict (LCP < 2.5s on 3G-class devices).
- Product photography initially comes from the Instagram import; must look premium even with phone-shot imagery.
- Currency NGN only. Fulfillment states: pending, paid, fulfilled, shipped, delivered.
